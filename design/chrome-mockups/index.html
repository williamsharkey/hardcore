Local, reversible reflow
Likely prose is joined by indentation and word boundaries. Try the existing rows first, including a short final line; then adjacent blank rows. At the left dock, move intersecting prose rows right by the menu width; before and after it, use full width. Code, tables, prompts and ambiguous boundaries are kept intact. Reflow must reduce collision with the fixed menu.
Text wins; hover reverses the order
No reserved column. No squeezing or automatic dock movement. Draw the menu dimly underneath; each nonblank terminal cell covers it. On hover, the whole menu comes forward. The subtle arrow and right-edge stem occupy a free indentation cell just left of changed text; no gutter is inserted.
